# The 30-Day Relaunch Plan
### EmptyNestMarriage.com — Billy & Maryruth Mitchell
*Goal: 5–10 couples served, 3+ real testimonials, 100+ warm contacts — then we relaunch the workshop to a room that's already full.*

---

## The One Rule for the Next 30 Days

**Stop marketing to strangers. Start inviting people you can name.**

No new website pages. No ads. No posting into the void. Every day this month, you're going to personally invite real people to real conversations. That's it. That's the plan.

---

# WEEK 1 — Build the List, Send the First Wave

### Day 1–2: The List of 25
Sit down with Maryruth and coffee. Write down **25 couples you actually know** who are empty nesters or close to it:
- Friends and former neighbors
- Church community (current and past churches)
- Family friends, siblings' friends
- Former coworkers
- That couple you always talk to at weddings

Rank them: warmest first. Don't overthink it — "would they answer my text?" is the bar.

### Day 3–5: Send the first 10 invitations (warmest first)
Send by text or Facebook DM — whichever channel you'd *normally* use with that person. Personal channels only. No BCC email blasts.

**📱 The Invitation (text/DM version):**
> Hey [name]! Billy here. So Maryruth and I finally did the thing we've been talking about for years — we started coaching empty-nest couples. Before we take on paying clients, we're doing a handful of free sessions to sharpen our process, and you two came to mind immediately. One hour on Zoom (or our porch), just the four of us, walking through one tool we use. No charge, no pitch, no weirdness — all we ask is your honest feedback afterward. Would you and [spouse] be up for it?

**📧 The Invitation (email version, for the less-texty folks):**
> Subject: A favor (the fun kind)
>
> Hey [name],
>
> Billy Mitchell here — it's been too long. Quick thing: Maryruth and I just launched something we've been dreaming about for years. We coach empty-nest couples — helping people our age stop feeling like roommates and start feeling like a couple again. (Turns out raising kids for 25 years is hard on a marriage. Who knew.)
>
> Before we take on paying clients, we're doing a small number of **free sessions** to fine-tune how we do this. You two were on the short list of couples we'd trust to give it to us straight.
>
> It's one hour, on Zoom or in person. We walk you through one of our tools, you tell us honestly what landed and what didn't. That's the whole deal — no charge, no sales pitch.
>
> Interested? Just reply and we'll find a time.
>
> — Billy (& Maryruth)

**Rules for this week:**
- Send 2–3 per day, not all at once. You want to actually schedule these, not drown.
- Personalize the first line of every single one. If you can't personalize it, they're not warm enough — move them down the list.

### Day 5–7: Also do these
- Paste the bio (from `bio.md`) into your Facebook Page "About" and Instagram bio.
- Post ONE simple announcement on your personal Facebook (not the business page — your *personal* profile, where actual humans see it):

**📣 The Personal Announcement Post:**
> Well, we did it. After years of "someday," Maryruth and I officially launched our couples coaching practice for empty nesters. 34 years married, kids launched, and we've learned a few things the hard way about falling back in love after the nest empties. If you know a couple staring at each other across a quiet dinner table wondering "now what?" — send them our way. EmptyNestMarriage.com 💛

*(Adjust the years-married number — I don't actually know it. But put the real number in; specificity builds trust.)*

**Week 1 finish line:** 10 invitations sent, 2–4 sessions booked, announcement posted.

# WEEK 2 — Run Sessions, Open the Church Door

### The free session itself (keep it simple)
One hour. Structure:
1. **10 min** — Their story. "Tell us about your empty nest moment."
2. **35 min** — Walk them through ONE tool from The Spark (the Reconnect conversation exercise works great live).
3. **10 min** — Their feedback: "What landed? What felt off? Would this have helped you two years ago?"
4. **5 min** — The two asks (below).

**🙏 Ask #1 — The Testimonial (say it exactly like this):**
> "If any part of this was helpful, would you write us 2–3 sentences we can put on our website? First names or initials only if you prefer."

**🔗 Ask #2 — The Referral:**
> "Who do you know that's staring down the empty nest right now? We've got a few more free spots and we'd love for you to hand one to a couple you care about."

### Day 8–10: Send invitations 11–20 from your list
Same scripts as Week 1.

### Day 10–14: Pastor/ministry outreach (send 3–5 of these)
Make a list of churches you have ANY connection to — your current church, past churches, churches of friends. The message:

**⛪ The Ministry Pitch (email or DM to pastor / marriage ministry leader):**
> Subject: A free evening for the married couples at [church name]
>
> Hi Pastor [name],
>
> Billy and Maryruth Mitchell here — [one line on your connection: "we've been attending since spring" / "our friends the Johnsons are longtime members"].
>
> We coach empty-nest couples — helping marriages thrive after the kids leave, with a faith foundation and a lot of laughter. We'd love to offer [church name] a **free evening event for married couples**: "The Empty Nest Date Night." One hour, one practical tool couples use that same night, zero cost to the church, and we're not selling anything from the stage.
>
> We know the season after the kids leave quietly strains a lot of good marriages in every congregation — and almost nobody's talking about it.
>
> Could we grab 15 minutes with you to see if this would serve your couples?
>
> Grace and peace,
> Billy & Maryruth Mitchell
> EmptyNestMarriage.com

**Week 2 finish line:** 20 total invitations sent, 3–5 sessions run, first testimonials in hand, 3–5 church pitches sent.

### Day 15–17: Put the proof on the site

- Post one testimonial on Facebook with a thank-you:

### Day 18–19: The gentle nudge (for non-responders)
About half your invitations will get no reply. That's normal — people are busy, not uninterested. One follow-up, ever:

**📱 The Nudge:**
> Hey [name] — no pressure at all, just floating this back up in case it got buried. The free spots are filling and I'd hate for you two to miss it if you wanted in. Either way, hope you're doing great!

### Day 19–21: Send invitations 21–25 + referral wave
Every couple who's done a session gets this one week later:

**📱 The Referral Follow-Up:**
> Hey [name]! Thanks again for being guinea pigs — you two genuinely helped us get better. Quick ask: we're opening 3 more free spots before we go fully paid. Is there a couple you'd want to hand one to? Just connect us by text and we'll take it from there.

**Week 3 finish line:** All 25 invited, 6–8 sessions done, 3+ testimonials live on the site, at least 1 church conversation scheduled.

# WEEK 4 — Count the Room, Set the Date

### Day 22–26: Finish strong
- Run remaining booked sessions.
- Follow up on church pitches (same nudge script, pastor version).
- Keep posting 2×/week on Facebook: one story/lesson post, one testimonial or behind-the-scenes post. (Drafts in `workshop-facebook-plan.md` still work — swap dates.)

### Day 27–28: Count your warm room
Add up:
- Email list subscribers (Kit)
- Couples who did sessions + their referrals
- Church contacts who said yes or maybe
- Engaged Facebook friends (people who commented/messaged, not just liked)

**The rule: 100+ warm contacts = announce the next workshop. Under 100 = run this playbook for two more weeks first.** A workshop announced to 100 warm people gets 20–30 registrations and 10–15 couples live. That's a great room. Under that, we're re-running the empty-room movie.

### Day 29–30: Plan the relaunch
When the math works:
- Pick a date 3 weeks out.
- The workshop page, registration system, reminder emails, and replay system are **already built and waiting** — we just update the date and Zoom link.
- Consider the smaller ask: "The Empty Nest Check-Up — 45 minutes, one tool, come as you are."
- First workshop email goes to your list (which now actually exists).
